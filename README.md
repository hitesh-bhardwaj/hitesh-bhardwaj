## 👋 About Me

I'm a **Frontend Engineer** based in **New Delhi, India** - passionate about building digital experiences that live at the intersection of design and engineering. I specialise in crafting high-performance, visually immersive web applications with a deep focus on animations, 3D interactions, and pixel-perfect UI.

From FLIP fluid simulations to enterprise SaaS platforms, I work across the full spectrum of frontend complexity - bringing both technical precision and creative thinking to every project.

- 🔭 Currently building interactive web experiences with **Next.js**, **Three.js** & **WebGL**
- 🌊 Passionate about **physics simulations**, **GLSL shaders**, and GPU-accelerated graphics
- 🎨 Strong eye for design — I obsess over motion, typography, and micro-interactions
- 🌍 Worked with clients across **India**, **Dubai**, **Africa**, and **Europe**
- 🔒 Most of my best work lives in **private repos** — see live projects below
